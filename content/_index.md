# Welcome

Projects & Practices in Physics — E&M (EMPCubed) is the second-semester course in
the P³ sequence, covering electricity and magnetism from charge, field, and
potential through circuit elements, magnetic fields and forces, and
electromagnetic induction. It follows the same project-based approach as the
first-semester course: students work in groups on modeling projects,
combining mathematical theory, conceptual understanding, and computational
tools, while instructors facilitate discussion rather than lecture.

This site collects the EMPCubed course notes, organized by week, and the worked
examples referenced throughout them, migrated from the original [MSU PERL
wiki](https://www.msuperl.org/wikis/pcubed/doku.php?id=184_notes).

Course materials were developed by Michigan State University's [Physics
Education Research Lab](https://perl.natsci.msu.edu/) and [Department of Physics
and Astronomy](https://www.pa.msu.edu/), drawing on physics education research and instructor
feedback. Content is available under a [Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
license.
